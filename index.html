Out in the world

  • Civic news · Live

    The Norwalk Sound

    A daily civic brief for Norwalk, Connecticut. It reads the city's meeting agendas, packets and minutes, local news, public filings, weather and tides, and publishes before breakfast, with a Sunday newsletter and a full Spanish edition.

    The public record here is public but scattered across portals nobody has time to read. The Sound puts it in one place. Anything interpretive is AI-drafted and held for human review before it publishes; the record itself is published verbatim; no names from police logs ever appear.

    Read The Norwalk Sound↗ (opens thenorwalksound.com)

  • Accountability · Live

    Nothing To See Here

    A running, sourced timeline of how license-plate readers, body cameras, AI report-writers and the rest of the "public-safety technology" market are actually going, and what the failures cost taxpayers. A counter in the corner, THE TAB, adds up the documented public money.

    The format is borrowed, with credit, from Web3 Is Going Just Great. Every entry cites its sources and nothing publishes until code has confirmed a fetched source supports the claim, the date and the dollar figure. The entries are deadpan on purpose; the facts carry the irony.

    Visit Nothing To See Here↗ (opens nothingtosee.fyi)

Built for a few people

  • Family archive · Private

    FamilyBot

    A private archive for two families: a family tree, people pages, recipes, photos and stories, built to the standard "could your grandmother use this on her phone, alone, on the first try?"

    Genealogy lives in spreadsheets and subscription sites. This puts the proved research, the scanned certificates and the recipes in one warm place, with every edit logged and every invite admin-only.

    No link: invite-only, by design.

  • Personal briefing · Private

    HealthIntel

    A news-intelligence briefing built for one reader. It ingests healthcare trade press, publisher newsletters and news feeds, clusters the same story across outlets, ranks by relevance and writes a daily brief and a weekly digest.

    It runs under one honesty rule: it may never state a fact that wasn't in the retrieved headline or blurb, and it says which outlet is covering what rather than pretending to have read the article.

    No link: single-reader tool behind a login.

  • Workplace tools · Internal

    Marketing Dashboard & Ad Sandbox

    Two browser-only tools for a college marketing team. The dashboard turns exported campaign CSVs from social, email and web into charts and branded PDF reports. The Ad Sandbox tests ad copy against fifteen synthetic student personas before it runs.

    No install, no server, no admin rights: they open from a shared drive by double-click, and the data never leaves the machine.

    No link: lives on an internal network drive.
